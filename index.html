About Me

I’m a Machine Learning Engineer based in Barcelona, Spain, with an MSc in Data Science from the Università degli Studi di Padova and a BSc in Mathematics from Hacettepe University. My work focuses on machine learning, deep learning, NLP, model evaluation, and building practical ML systems.

I currently work at IREC, the Catalonia Institute for Energy Research, where I develop and benchmark machine-learning and deep-learning forecasting solutions for international R&D projects. My work includes building reproducible data pipelines, evaluating models across real-world datasets, and developing ML services using FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, and Linux-based virtual machine deployment.

Previously, I worked as an Artificial Intelligence Engineer Intern at FOREO, where I developed a multilingual AI application combining machine translation, automatic speech recognition, text-to-speech, and OCR. I also worked on Transformer-based model adaptation using LoRA, multilingual training data preparation, model evaluation, and API-based integration.

My technical interests include deep learning, Transformer models, NLP, model fine-tuning, efficient machine learning, and model optimization. I enjoy research-driven problem solving, comparing different modeling approaches, and translating experimental ideas into reliable implementations.

My background in mathematics strongly influences how I approach machine learning problems, particularly when working with model evaluation, optimization, and quantitative analysis. I primarily work with Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face Transformers, scikit-learn, FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL, and the broader scientific Python ecosystem.

Outside of machine learning, I enjoy photography, travel, and exploring different cultures and places.
